GameSync Launcher - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

GameSync Launcher is a personal, non-commercial application designed to synchronize video game save files between devices using Google Drive.

2. Information We Access

GameSync Launcher uses Google OAuth authentication to access Google Drive files authorized by the user.

The application may access, upload, download, and manage game save backups stored in Google Drive.

3. How Information Is Used

Google Drive access is used exclusively to synchronize, back up, and restore video game save files across authorized devices.

4. Data Storage

Game save backups are stored locally on the user's devices and in the user's Google Drive account.

OAuth authentication credentials are stored locally by the synchronization software and are not intentionally transmitted to independent application servers.

5. Data Sharing

GameSync Launcher does not sell personal data. Game save files are not shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Google Drive processes stored files according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users retain control over their game save backups and may delete them from their local devices and Google Drive.

Users may revoke the application's Google Drive permissions through their Google Account settings.

7. Security

GameSync Launcher uses Google's OAuth authorization system and secure HTTPS connections for Google Drive communication.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the application developer using the support email associated with the application's Google OAuth configuration.
